# 🙏 The Mount of Mercy | ደብረ ምሕረት

This application is designed to help bring Orthodox Christians and catechumens towards a life of unceasing prayer. It provides access to essential daily prayers, *Theotokia*, Psalms, and the Divine Liturgy (*Kidasé*) with lectionary readings in multiple languages, including **Amharic, Coptic, English, French, Ge'ez, Spanish, and Tigrinya** with phonetic transliterations to assist in learning and pronunciation.

## 📖 Translations & Sources

> “Action, then, comes by faith, as ‘if you do not believe’, Isaiah says, ‘you will not understand’ (Isaiah 7:9); and the truth brings about faith, for faith is established upon things truly real, that we may believe what really is, as it is, and believing what really is, as it is, we may always keep our conviction of it firm. Since, then, the conserver of our salvation is faith, it is necessary to take great care of it, that we may have a true comprehension of what is.”
>
> — Saint Irenaeus, Bishop of Lyons (*“On the Apostolic Preaching”* Preface, Section 3, c. 180 A.D.)

The English translations of the Daily Prayer and *Theotokia* in this app are a work-in-progress and not yet formally approved by clergy. They are rendered through a process of careful harmonization of the extant English, Ge'ez, Coptic, Amharic, Arabic, and Greek texts (listed below)—preferring to render the literal Ge'ez meaning in plain English wherever possible, so that we may simply 'believe what really is, as it is.' Upon completion, God willing, we hope to offer the Daily Prayer and *Theotokia* translations compiled in this app to our Bishop, Diocesan Fathers, and Scholars for formal approval.

## ✨ Features

### Five-Tab Navigation & Ethiopic Calendar Header

* **Header Liturgical Bar:** Displays the live Ethiopian liturgical hour, Ethiopic calendar date (*Bahire Hasab* computation), daily/annual feast commemorations, and an interactive date picker to preview or override readings for any liturgical day.
* **Five Core Modes:** Switch seamlessly between **Pray** (daily & personal prayer presets), **Liturgy** (*Kidasé* with appointed lectionary readings), **Learn** (Ge'ez Interlinear & Sunday School activities), **Targuma (Beta)** (live sermon captioning & translation), and **Search** (instant search across prayers and Scriptures).

### Liturgical & Daily Prayer Modules (Right Sidebar)

* **Daily Prayer (`ዘዘወትር ጸሎት`):** Foundational daily prayers of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church with customizable intercessory names.
* **Daily Lectionary (`ምንባባት ዘለለዕለቱ`):** Complete 1938 AD EOTC daily lectionary (*Katameros*) with Morning, Evening, and Divine Liturgy Scripture appointments, festal reading-set selectors, and original 1938 page scan inspection.
* **Sunset / Vespers (`ጸሎተ ሠርክ`) & Morning / Matins (`ጸሎተ ነግህ`):** Configurable with Evening and Morning Psalm (*Misbak*) and Gospel lectionary pickers.
* **_Tselote Se’atat_ Readings (`ምንባባተ ሰዓታት`):** Appointed daily Scripture readings for the Prayer of the Hours according to the certified tradition of *Debre Abay Abune Samuel Ze-Waldiba* Monastery.
* **Pre-Liturgy Psalms (`መዝሙር ዘዳዊት ዘቅድመ ቅዳሴ`):** Appointed preparatory Psalms recited before the Divine Liturgy (LXX Psalms 24, 60, 101, 102, 129, 130).
* **Crowning / Holy Matrimony (`ሥርዓተ ተክሊል`):** Matrimonial service with dynamic insertion of the Bridegroom's and Bride's baptismal names.
* **Order of the Liturgy (`ሥርዓተ ቅዳሴ`) & Eucharistic Prayer (`አኰቴተ ቍርባን`):**
  * **Liturgical Customizations:** Select the Day of *Kidasé* (Sunday/Major Feasts, Saturday/First Sabbath, or Weekday Mon–Fri), Time of the Covenant Prayer (*Kidan* at Dawn, Midnight, or Sunset), Quiet Prayers (`ጸሎት በልኁሳስ`), Holy Fifty Days (`እምትንሣኤ እስከ በዓለ ፶`), and Ge'ez Phonetics for Chants.
  * **Interactive Lectionary Pickers:** Select the Pauline Epistle, Universal Epistle (with dynamic author attribution), Acts of the Apostles, Liturgy Psalm (*Misbak*, with a searchable *Typical Liturgy Psalm Chants* modal), and Liturgy Gospel—accessible from both the Right Sidebar and inline on Liturgy cards.
  * **Anaphoras & _Melk'a_:** Includes the Anaphora of the Apostles (`ዘሐዋርያት`) and the Anaphora of Our Lady Mary (`ዘእግዝእትነ ማርያም`), integrated with *Melk'a Qurban* (`መልክአ ቍርባን`).
  * **Miracles of Mary (`ተአምረ ማርያም`):** Browse, search, filter, or **identify by voice** stories from the PEMM dataset to insert after the Anaphora or at Matins.
* **Theotokia & Marian Hymns:**
  * 7-day *Praise of Mary* (`ውዳሴ ማርያም`) in Ge'ez and Ge'ez phonetics with ongoing multi-lingual translations (including Monday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday English/Amharic layers), plus *The Gate of Light* (`አንቀጸ ብርሃን`), *The Angels Praise Mary* (`ይዌድስዋ መላእክት ለማርያም`), and *Supplications* (`ምሕላ`).

### Learn Module & Targuma Live Captioning

* **Ge'ez Interlinear Explorer:** Word-by-word BibleHub-style morphological, transliteration, and lexical breakdown of liturgical Ge'ez prayers.
* **Foundation of Life Sunday School Classes:** Interactive learning games organized across three parish class tiers—**Hitsanat** (Under 7), **St. Arsema's Class** (Ages 7–9), and **St. John's Class** (Ages 10–12, including dynamic 5×5 Lectionary Jeopardy generated from the day's readings)—with full URL deep-link support.
* **Targuma Live Captioning (Beta):** Real-time speech recognition and translation for parish sermons and readings, plus microphone-assisted *Miracles of Mary* story matching.

### Multi-Edition Bible & Psalter Engine

* **Global & Per-Reading Bible Edition Selector:** Configure global defaults in the Left Sidebar and override translations per individual reading in the Right Sidebar:
  * **English:** `NKJV1982` (New King James Version) and `KJV1611` (King James Version), with Septuagint (`OSB`) Psalm numbering cited alongside Masoretic numbering.
  * **Ge'ez (`ግእዝ`):** `ግእዝ1980 (Septuagint)` (Psalms & Songs of the Prophets), `ግእዝ1980 (Masoretic)` (89-book broader EOTC canon including *Jubilees*, *Enoch*, 1–3 Ethiopic *Meqabyan*, *Josippon*, and all 8 Apostolic Church Order books), `ግእዝ2014` (2022 AD New Testament), `ግእዝ1694` (1701 AD Ludolf Psalter), and `ግእዝ1845` (1853 AD Dillmann Octateuch).
  * **Amharic (`አማርኛ`):** `አም2000` (2000 E.C. / 2007 AD Millennium 89-book broader canon Bible) and `አም1954` (1954 E.C. / 1962 AD Haile Selassie I 66-book Bible).
  * **Tigrinya (`ትግርኛ`):** `1997 ዓ.ም.` (2004–2005 AD Tigrinya Bible).
  * **Spanish (`Español`):** `2010 RVG` (Reina Valera Gómez).
  * **Coptic (`ϯⲙⲉⲧⲣⲉⲙⲛ̀ⲭⲏⲙⲓ`):** `2025 Bohairic` (Coptic Scriptorium Bohairic Psalter).
* **Typical Psalm Combinations & Custom Sequences:** Choose from traditional EOTC monastic, *Yaredawi*, royal (*Nigus*), catechetical/mystagogical (St. Cyril of Jerusalem), Penance, Chrismation, Coptic *Agpeya*, and feast-day Psalm combinations (supporting verse ranges), or enter a custom Psalm sequence order.
* **📜 St. Athanasius’ Guide to the Psalms:** Searchable prescription modal based on St. Athanasius' *Letter to Marcellinus on the Interpretation of the Psalms* (c. 360 AD), filterable by spiritual trial, emotion, prophecy, or category.

### Display, Historical Archives & Sharing (Left Sidebar)

* **Language Selection & Ordering:** Toggle any combination of languages, drag or reorder language display priority, enable language color-coding (Soft, Bright, Grey), and switch between row and column layouts.
* **Customizable Fonts & Themes:** Traditional, Brown (Sepia), Ocean, Legacy, and Simple (High-Contrast) palettes in Light and Dark modes, traditional *Hareg* vine headers, rubricated Holy Names, and independent or locked Ge'ez and English font sizing.
* **Presentation Mode:** Full-screen **Slides** or **Scroll** mode for parish projection or personal prayer, featuring dynamic font scaling and slide transitions.
* **Rich Historical & Iconographic Modals:**
  * **Historical *Yä-Betä Krəstiyan Ṣälot* Prefaces (1931 & 1938 AD):** Readable in English, original Amharic, or Side-by-Side view.
  * **Eusebius Psalm Arrangement (c. 335 AD):** Multi-lingual modal (English, Ge'ez, Greek) with 14th-century Mt. Sinai manuscript imagery.
  * **St. Ephrem the Syrian Potter:** 7-day narrative modal from PEMM Manuscript ID 14.
  * **Sacred Icon Metadata:** Inspection of Ethiopian manuscript illuminations with historical descriptions, sources, and biblical references.
* **Share Link & Share Link with Scriptures:** Copy short links to your current prayers and settings, or copy a link together with all selected Liturgy Scripture readings.
* **Scribe's Chamber:** Authenticated Firebase editor for authorized scribes to refine prayer texts and icon metadata.

## 💻 Technical Overview

* **Dual Web & Native Android Architecture:** Built with React + TypeScript (Vite) shell components paired with shared liturgical JavaScript/CSS engines, packaged both as an offline-capable **Progressive Web App (PWA)** and a native **Android Application** (`app.mountofmercy.prayer`) serving bundled assets via `WebViewAssetLoader` with edge-to-edge support.
* **Testing & Cryptographic Dataset Integrity:** Verified by an automated **Vitest + Happy-DOM** test suite (`npm test`) and `npm run verify:integrity` SHA-256 checksum validation protecting all liturgical and scriptural datasets against accidental corruption.
* **Backend Services:** Uses **Firebase (Authentication & Firestore)** for short-link state persistence and the Scribe's Chamber, deployed via Google Cloud Run and GitHub Actions.
